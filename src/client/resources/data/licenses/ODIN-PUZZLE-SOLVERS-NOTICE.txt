Third-party behavior port: dungeon puzzle solver behavior

Source repository: https://github.com/odtheking/Odin
Pinned revision: 833e0533ef9c47529b790612627a65618ebd5a58 (Odin 0.3.6)
Reviewed source: puzzle solver settings, detection, event/timer flow, rendering,
progression, reset behavior, and catalog resources.

solver implementation source is included by this port.
