GeilerAddons redistributes a modified subset in
../geileraddons/devonian-dungeon-waypoints.json. The source's room-relative integer coordinates
and category distinctions are retained. Rows are flattened to one marker per record and assigned
stable local IDs. No coordinates were authored or inferred by GeilerAddons.

The subset contains 452 records across 121 local marker names. All 120 source rows are mapped by
115 exact room names, the punctuation-only alias "Criss Cross" to local "Criss-Cross", three
cross-checked name aliases ("Silvers Sword" to "Silver Sword", "Mini Rail Track" to "Rail Track",
and "Pillars" to "Rare Pillars"), and the generic "Blaze" row split into local "Higher Blaze" and
"Lower Blaze" using independently labeled matching chest coordinates from Skyblocker. Core hashes
were not used to make these joins.

  chest:   238 records in 108 rooms
  item:     74 records in 58 rooms
  essence:  35 records in 30 rooms
  bat:      49 records in 43 rooms
  redstone:  4 records in 3 rooms
  lever:    52 records in 45 rooms

Thirteen of the 121 local marker names have no chest records. This is partial coverage and does not
establish that every room or secret is represented.
